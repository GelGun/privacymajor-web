Services

How we can help

Each service can be ordered on its own or as part of a long-term engagement.

01

GDPR audit and implementation

We map what data you process and why. You receive records of processing activities, a list of risks and a concrete remediation plan.

02

Data Protection Officer

We act as your organisation's Data Protection Officer (DPO), including communication with the supervisory authority and with data subjects.

03

Documentation and contracts

Privacy notices, information for employees and customers, data processing agreements, transfers of data outside the EU.

04

Cookies, websites and marketing

We review your website and set up the cookie banner and consents so that they meet the law and the practice of the Czech DPA.

05

Incidents and DPA proceedings

We help you handle a data breach within the 72-hour deadline and guide you through inspections and proceedings before the authority.

06

Training and ongoing support

Training for management and staff, and a helpdesk for everyday questions with answers within two working days.

How we work

A clear process, a fixed price, one point of contact

  1. Initial consultation

    In 30 minutes we find out what you need and how fast. The consultation is free of obligation.

  2. Proposal and price

    You receive a written scope of work, a deadline and a price you know in advance.

  3. Delivery

    We work with your people and systems. The result is documentation you can actually use.

  4. Long-term care

    We follow changes in legislation and case law and tell you in time what affects you.
